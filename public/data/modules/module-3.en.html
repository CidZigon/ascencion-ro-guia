⚙️ Schwartzvald — Rekenber and the price of progress
An industrial investigation ends up revealing human experiments and a political fight against Rekenber.
Factory QuestEinbroch MurderBiolabs EntranceCursed SpiritPresident QuestOdin's Temple ExcavationRebellion

🌱 Beginning

It all starts in Einbroch with seemingly ordinary problems: a poorly maintained factory and a murder investigation. The clues start pointing to Rekenber and the Republic's interest in materials related to Ymir.

⚔️ Development

In Lighthalzen, the adventurer enters Biolabs and discovers the darker side of Rekenber's research: test subjects, victims, and experiments that can no longer be hidden as simple scientific progress. The investigation escalates all the way to the Schwartzvald government and also reaches expeditions at Odin's Temple.

🏁 Outcome

The Rebellion Quest brings that evidence and those contacts together in an attempt to stop Rekenber's operations. The corporation doesn't disappear, but it becomes clear that Schwartzvald's progress has a human cost, and that part of the government is willing to confront it.

Rekenber links Schwartzvald's prosperity to its experiments, Biolabs, and the political resistance against the corporation.

☀️ Arunafeltz — the crisis within the faith
A minor disappearance leads to the political and religious heart of Rachel.
Lost ChildRachel SanctuaryVeins SiblingsThor Volcano BasePeace for Arunafeltz

🌱 Beginning

Vincent is looking for a missing child. The Lost Child investigation seems like a minor problem, but it ends up bringing the adventurer closer to High Priest Zhed and the internal affairs of Rachel's temple.

⚔️ Development

Rachel Sanctuary reveals divisions within the Church of Freyja. The investigation continues toward Veins and Thor Volcano, where the consequences of those decisions stop being merely political and start directly affecting ordinary people.

🏁 Outcome

Peace for Arunafeltz tries to close the crisis from within: Zhed, Niren, and the Pope must face what happened and find a way to keep the institution from continuing down the same path.

The conflict shows a church divided between factions, political interests, and priests trying to make amends for what happened.

⛪ Nameless Island — what they tried to erase
The secrets of Rune-Midgard and Arunafeltz end up converging on an abandoned island.
Curse of GaebolgRachel SanctuaryVeins SiblingsNameless Island Entrance

🌱 Beginning

Curse of Gaebolg investigates historical secrets of Rune-Midgard, while Rachel Sanctuary and Veins Siblings leave unresolved questions in Arunafeltz. Different clues end up pointing to the same place.

⚔️ Development

Agents, priests, and contacts in Veins lead the adventurer toward an island that has been set apart from the normal routes. There they find a destroyed community and the Cursed Monastery, overrun by the undead and demonic creatures.

🏁 Outcome

The investigation culminates inside the monastery, where Fallen Bishop Hibram and the horrors of the place reveal just how far the decisions that were meant to stay hidden actually went.

The story ties together the threads of Rachel, Veins, and Curse of Gaebolg before leading to the Cursed Monastery.

🔥 Morroc — the fall of a city
The ancient seal on Satan Morroc fails and turns the heart of the desert into a dimensional wound.
Dandelion's Request / variantContinental Guard / variantSatan Morroc

🌱 Beginning

Morroc exists on top of an ancient threat: Satan Morroc had been sealed within the region, and the city grew up around the place meant to contain him.

⚔️ Development

The investigations and forces gathered around Morroc end up confronting the demon's return. The city is devastated and the terrain is left warped by the Dimensional Gorge.

🏁 Outcome

Defeating or containing Satan Morroc doesn't restore the city. The catastrophe leaves a breach into an unknown region and directly sets up the expedition to the New World.

The exact quest can vary depending on the server's implementation; the central event is the fall of Morroc and the opening of the Dimensional Gorge.

🌌 New World — the expedition
The three great powers cooperate to explore the territory beyond the Dimensional Gorge.
Onward to the New WorldNew SurroundingsAttitude to the New WorldReport from the New WorldFinding a Fairy

🌱 Beginning

Rune-Midgarts, Schwartzvald, and Arunafeltz form a joint expedition. The adventurer passes through the different nations before receiving authorization to cross the Dimensional Gorge.

⚔️ Development

Midgard Camp becomes the exploration base. The first quests study the wildlife, resources, routes, and conditions of the place. Report from the New World gathers that information and sends it back to the human nations.

🏁 Outcome

Finding a Fairy confirms the territory isn't empty: there are peoples who already lived there long before the expedition arrived.

The expedition changes the scale of the story: the three nations stop exploring each other and start studying an unknown territory.

🌿 Laphine and Sapha — learning to listen
The most important problem in the New World isn't killing monsters — it's being able to communicate with its inhabitants.
Ring of the Wise KingTwo Tribes

🌱 Beginning

The expedition members encounter the Laphine and Sapha, but can't speak with them. Ring of the Wise King grows out of the attempt to understand their languages and creates a way to communicate.

⚔️ Development

With the ring, the adventurer begins dealing directly with Arc, Snorren, and other members of both cultures. Two Tribes shows that their conflicts have real causes: territory, survival, resources, and problems surrounding Yggdrasil.

🏁 Outcome

The player stops being an outside observer and becomes a mediator between humans, the Laphine, and the Sapha.

The Ring of the Wise King lets the Laphine and Sapha stop being incomprehensible inhabitants and become direct conversation partners.

🌳 Yggdrasil — the close of Episode 13.2
The investigations by humans, the Laphine, and the Sapha all end up leading to the roots of the great tree.
Two TribesGuardian of YggdrasilNidhoggur's Nest

🌱 Beginning

Two Tribes leads the adventurer to Yggdrasil Dungeon and makes it clear there's a deeper problem surrounding the tree.

⚔️ Development

Guardian of Yggdrasil gathers information from Midgard Camp, Prontera, the Laphine, and the Sapha. The investigation requires securing cooperation between peoples who, until recently, couldn't even communicate.

🏁 Outcome

Access to Nidhoggur's Nest and the confrontation with Nidhoggur's Shadow serve as the natural climax of the content we're covering up to Episode 13.2.

Guardian of Yggdrasil and Nidhoggur's Nest bring the narrative line covered up to Episode 13.2 to its conclusion.

🌑 The Sign — Geffenia
A long archaeological investigation that ends up opening a route to Geffenia.
Umbala LanguagePiano KeysThe Sign

🌱 Beginning

The Sign begins with trials and fragments related to Sobbing Starlight. Little by little, the search leads toward Umbala and Niflheim.

⚔️ Development

The adventurer gathers pieces, interprets documents, learns to communicate where language is a barrier, and completes a series of trials that connect the present with ancient stories.

🏁 Outcome

In the end they obtain Lucifer's Lament, which opens the portal to Geffenia.

The Sign ties together archaeology, Umbala, Niflheim, and ancient clues until it opens the way to Geffenia.

Kiel Hyre explores identity, technology, and the consequences of creating beings capable of acting and remembering.
